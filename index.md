<h1>ALPhA @ Davidson College</h1>

<p>
Welcome to the Algorithms for Learning in Physics Applications (ALPhA) research group at Davidson College, led by Dr. Michelle Kuchera and Dr. Raghu Ramanujan. We develop <strong>deep learning</strong> methods for <strong>nuclear physics</strong> and <strong>particle physics</strong>, working closely with experimentalists at the Facility for Rare Isotope Beams (FRIB) and other national laboratories and universities. Undergraduate researchers play a central role in our work.
</p>

<hr />

<h2>What we're working on</h2>

<div class="team-grid">
  <div class="team-card">
    <h3>Representation Learning for TPC Data</h3>
    <p>Reusable representations of time projection chamber data. Our recent work examines how model architecture and training shape these embeddings across tasks, experiments, and detectors, including the AT-TPC and GADGET II.</p>
    <p><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21756">Recent preprint</a> · <a href="https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/showAward?AWD_ID=2311263">NSF Award #2311263</a></p>
  </div>

  <div class="team-card">
    <h3>Bridging Simulation and Experiment</h3>
    <p>Generative and diffusion models that translate between simulated and measured detector data, adding realistic detector response to simulations or removing noise from measurements.</p>
    <p><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.18674">NeurIPS ML4PS paper</a></p>
  </div>

  <div class="team-card">
    <h3>Finding Rare Events in the AT-TPC</h3>
    <p>A point-cloud model trained on heuristically labeled data that identifies rare fission events a hand-tuned method left unclassified. Together, the two approaches identify about 99% of fission events.</p>
    <p><a href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nima.2024.170002">NIM A paper</a></p>
  </div>
</div>

<p>Earlier ALPhA projects include machine-learning event generators and QCD analysis with Jefferson Lab and jet emulation for the CMS experiment at CERN. See our <a href="{{ '/publications' | relative_url }}">publications</a> for more.</p>

<h2>Interested in ALPhA?</h2>
<p>Are you a student interested in working with us? We welcome Davidson students from all areas of study who are curious about the intersection of AI and science. There are potential opportunities for non-Davidson undergraduates. Reach out to Dr. Michelle Kuchera or Dr. Raghu Ramanujan to learn more.</p>

<p>Are you a physics researcher looking for potential collaboration? We are enthusiastic about collaborating on projects that we find exciting from a machine learning perspective. Please read <a href="{{ '/collaborate' | relative_url }}">Collaborating with ALPhA</a>, then reach out to Michelle or Raghu to discuss your project.</p>
